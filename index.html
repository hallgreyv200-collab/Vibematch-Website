VibeMatch Terminology

Everything you need to know to master the platform.

⚡

Sparks

Your daily utility token. Earn them for free by watching ads or inviting friends. Use them to extend matching limits, pin locations on the Radar, or unlock premium private chat features.

🍸

Vibe Coins

The premium creator currency. Used exclusively to purchase live stream access, buy AR screen doodles, or send high-tier Tributes (gifts) to your favorite broadcasters.

🎙️

120s Micro-Date

Before diving into endless texting, matched users jump into a quick 2-minute voice or video call. If both click "Yes" after the timer, full messaging unlocks.

🤝

RSVP Vault

Eliminates flaking. When proposing a real-world date, stake a small amount of Sparks. Both users get their Sparks refunded (plus a bonus) when they GPS check-in at the venue together.

🗺️

360° Pulse Radar

A real-time, interactive city map. View spontaneous "Flash" meetups, local verified events, and active groups around your location.

👯‍♂️

Wingman Squad Mode

Link accounts with a friend to swipe the network as a duo. Perfect for organizing safe, group outings and double dates.
